# HaMachberet word list.
#
# One word per line, pipe separated:
#   n. | hebrew with nikud | transliteration | english | pos[.gender] | themes | extra
#
# pos:     noun verb adj phrase connector grammar number other
# gender:  .m .f .pl .mf   (nouns and numbers only; a noun must have one)
# themes:  comma separated, from the list in tools/build-vocab.py
# extra:   key=value pairs joined by ;   known keys: root, binyan, pl
#
# Hebrew is written in full spelling with nikud, so that taking the points
# off leaves exactly what a learner types: שׁוּלְחָן, not שֻׁלְחָן.
# Adjectives are masculine / feminine. Verbs are the infinitive.
#
# Build with:  python3 tools/build-vocab.py
